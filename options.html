        A PLAN FOR THE NEXT EPISODE

        How it works

        01

        Set your daily plan

        Choose an episode limit and a playback-minute limit. An episode you’ve started can finish within your episode allowance; the minute limit can pause it partway through. Paused time and seeking don’t add minutes.

        02

        Pause before continuing

        At your first limit, stop for today or wait 60 seconds to watch another episode. The button counts down until it’s available.

        03

        Make one final choice

        After that extra episode, continuing requires a five-minute pause and typing “I want to watch one more episode”. You can always choose to stop instead.

        04

        Leave the rest for tomorrow

        After the second extra episode, Netflix is locked until local midnight. Choosing “Stop for today” also locks Netflix for the day.

        What does an extra episode cover?

        If the minute limit pauses your current video, continuing lets that video finish. At an episode boundary, it permits the next video. Both daily limits allow that one video to finish. Moving to a different video ends that permission.

        If Netflix hasn’t supplied a title, the permission still follows that watch page. Movies contribute minutes; their continuation covers the rest of the current movie.

        Where are my settings and history stored?

        Everything stays in this browser’s local extension storage. The popup and this page use the same saved settings. Daily totals reset at local midnight, and up to 90 recorded days remain available here. No account or separate website is involved.

        If your browser or computer sleeps through midnight, the lock clears after it wakes and the extension runs. Reload any previously blocked Netflix page. Removing the extension removes its local data.
